Note:

  • The columns are separated by tabs.
  • The first column is ignored.
  • The new plate set will have plates with the barcodes specified in the second column.
  • The well addresses of the new plates are specified in the third and fourth columns.
  • The agar plates must already exist in Genedata Biologics. Their barcodes are listed in the fifth column.
  • The resulting plate set is a master plate set, and new antibody clone IDs are generated for each well of each plate.
