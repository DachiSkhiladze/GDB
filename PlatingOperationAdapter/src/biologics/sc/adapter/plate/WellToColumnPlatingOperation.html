Create new plates from an existing plate. Each antibody clone is plated into an entire column of wells on the new plate. For example, the first clone from well A1 on an initial plate is plated into wells A-H in column 1 of a new 96-well plate. The second antibody clone, from well A2, is plated into wells A-H of column 2 of the new 96-well plate, an so on. Based on the plate layout, antibody clones are not placed in wells reserved, for example, for controls.
