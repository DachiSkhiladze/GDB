Arrange the contents of source plates on bigger destination plates. For each set of four source plates, one destination plate is generated. This means either four 96-well plates go to one 384-well plate, or four 24-well plates go to one 96-well plate.

For example, four 96-well plates are transferred to a 384-well plate as so:

  • Wells A1, A2, B1, and B2 are mapped from the first (A1) well of each of the four 96-well plates.
  • Wells C1, C2, D1, and D2 are mapped from the second (B1) well of each of the four 96-well plates.
  • And so on, until finally wells O23, O24, P23, and P24 are mapped from the last (H12) well of each of the four 96-well plates.
